## 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create an immediately understandable PERSONAL HELPER / FRIENDLY ASSISTANT Android app icon for a Korean app that helps people understand and use their phone. Viewer should instantly feel "my helpful companion I can ask", not an abstract corporate brand, messenger app, customer call center, or chatbot technology product. A personable face is REQUIRED. Inclusive across ages, approachable and respectful, not babyish or a toy. Distinctive polished flat illustration/logo hybrid, strong silhouette, large simple features readable at 32px. Square canvas with full-bleed pale lavender #DEE0FF background. Main shapes deep blue-purple #4A5FE0, white #FFFFFF and deep navy #00105C facial details. Mark comfortably within central 65% safe area. No words, letters, tiny details, gradients, texture, shadow, glow, watermark, sparkles, headphones, microphone, antenna, gears, arrows or focus brackets. One icon only. Clearly designed memorable friendly character; don't draw realistic humans. Concept 1: Talking companion. A single substantial rounded WHITE speech bubble is itself a friendly helper character, with two large deep-blue oval eyes and a small broad curved smile, one short clear speech tail lower-left. The bubble has a thick blue-purple exterior contour and a softly squared silhouette. Add one tiny-in-count but large-in-size raised blue rounded arm emerging from its right side, with a simple mitten-like open hand, greeting the user. No fingers or fine detail. Warm calm expressive face, not grinning cartoon. Integrate everything into one compact clean silhouette. The character should look available to ask for help.
